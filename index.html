Which service should I use?

911

911 Behavioral Health Response

For situations requiring a rapid response

Some North Carolina communities have specialized behavioral health teams that can be dispatched through 911 for eligible calls. These programs may respond without police depending on the situation and local program.

If someone is in immediate danger or needs emergency medical attention, call 911.

MCT

Mobile Crisis Team

Behavioral health crisis assessment and support

Mobile Crisis Teams can travel to a person experiencing a mental health or substance use crisis. They provide assessment, de-escalation, and help determine next steps for treatment.

Mobile Crisis Teams are available 24/7 throughout North Carolina, but response times vary and may be two hours or longer.

This resource is intended to help identify local behavioral health crisis services. Program availability and service areas may change.

For an immediate life-threatening emergency, call 911.
